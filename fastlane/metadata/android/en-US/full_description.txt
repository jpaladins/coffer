Coffer opens your VeraCrypt® containers on Android, without root and without internet access.

OPEN
• Password, PIM and keyfiles, hidden volumes too.
• Every VeraCrypt cipher: AES, Serpent, Twofish, Camellia, Kuznyechik and their cascades; SHA-512, SHA-256, Whirlpool, BLAKE2s, Streebog and Argon2id.
• Quick fingerprint unlock, if you choose to remember the password.

BROWSE AND EDIT
• List or grid view, with photo previews made in memory only.
• Search and sort by name, date or size.
• Import files, create folders, rename, move and delete, several files at once too, on FAT and exFAT.
• Open files with other apps without leaving plaintext copies on your phone.
• Hidden volume protection while you write to the outer one.

CREATE
• New FAT or exFAT containers in a few steps, which VeraCrypt on your computer opens as usual.

SECURITY AND PRIVACY
• Automatic container lock, app lock with PIN or fingerprint, screenshots blocked.
• No internet permission, no ads, no tracking.
• Light, dark or system theme.
• Free software under the GPLv3: the code is public and can be checked.

Coffer is in beta: it works and is verified with VeraCrypt, but always keep a backup copy of your containers.
